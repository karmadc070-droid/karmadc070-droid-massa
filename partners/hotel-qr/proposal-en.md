# massa – In-room massage partnership (for hotels and serviced apartments)

**massa** is a booking app that sends verified massage and beauty therapists to guest rooms across Vietnam (app.massaviet.com, Android and iPhone apps).
Place one QR card in each room. Guests scan to book, and every completed booking earns your property a commission.

## What your property gets
- **A new guest service and extra revenue.** Offer in-room massage with no spa room and no staff. For every completed booking from a guest who came through your QR code, your property earns **[commission rate TBD — current system default 10%]** of what the guest paid.
- **No operational work.** Booking, therapist dispatch, payment and guest support all happen in the massa app. Your front desk only lets the therapist in.
- **Transparent tracking.** Linked guests, bookings and earned amounts are recorded per property code in the massa admin console. Payout schedule: **[TBD]**.

## How it works
1. massa prints a QR card with your property's own code (A6 table stand, Korean, Vietnamese and English).
2. Place the card in rooms or the lobby.
3. When a guest scans and signs in, their account is linked to your property. Bookings made within 30 days of linking count for your property.
4. When booking, the guest enters the hotel name, room number and how to get past reception. For properties that require visitor registration, the app says so at the search step.
5. The guest pays on the spot after the service (card, MoMo · ZaloPay QR, or cash). Displayed prices include tax and tip.

## Why guests feel safe
- **Verified badge.** Given only to therapists who pass three checks: certificate review, ID verification and an in-person interview.
- **Hygiene certification.** Given to partners confirmed to use single-use hygiene kits.
- **Safety button.** If anything goes wrong during a session, the guest's location goes to an administrator immediately.
- **Real reviews.** Only guests who used the service can rate and review.
- **Zero tolerance for inappropriate requests.** This applies to both sides, and violations lead to immediate suspension.
- **5 languages.** Korean, Vietnamese, English, Japanese and Chinese. Guests can choose a therapist who speaks their language.
- **Sessions.** 60 · 90 · 120 minutes. Aroma, Swedish, Thai, deep tissue, hot stone, foot, head, back, neck and shoulder, sports, cupping and no-oil.

massa services are for relaxation and care, not medical treatment.
